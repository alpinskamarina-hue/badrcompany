What's in the price?

Hourly rate

€ 50 per hour excluding VAT. For private customers that is € 54.50 including 9% VAT (plastering and painting in homes older than 2 years) or € 60.50 including 21% VAT (other work). The calculator uses average working times per m². The quote contains a fixed price, not an open-ended bill.

VAT

Labour for plastering and painting in a home older than 2 years is 9% VAT. Other work, such as tiles, floors and drywall partitions, is 21%. Read more.

Materials

Materials are listed separately in the quote. You can buy them yourself or we order them for you, without hidden mark-up.

Travel costs

Included, anywhere in the Netherlands. For work far outside the Zwolle region we agree in advance how we plan the travel time.

Minimum

We charge a minimum of 4 hours of labour per job. We're happy to combine small jobs in one visit.

Extra work

Only with your approval. If we come across something, you get photos and a price first.
