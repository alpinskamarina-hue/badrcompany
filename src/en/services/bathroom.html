Questions about bathrooms

How long will I be without a bathroom?

For an average bathroom, allow two to three weeks, depending on drying times and delivery of fixtures. We plan this with you in advance so you know where you can shower.

Can the fixtures stay in the same place?

That's the cheapest option, because the drains stay where they are. Moving them is possible too, but needs more plumbing and sometimes a different floor build-up.

What if something turns out to be wrong behind the tiles?

Then we stop first, send you photos and a price, and only continue after your approval. So there are no surprises on the bill.
