What we do

Colour with crisp edges

Good paintwork depends on preparation: sanding, filling, degreasing and careful masking. Only then does the paint go on.

  • Walls and ceilings in latex or wall paint, matt or satin
  • Priming and painting new plaster
  • Lacquering frames, doors and skirting
  • Treating stains and old paint layers first
  • Colour advice based on light and space

How we work

Our approach

  1. Protect

    Floor covered, furniture wrapped, sockets and edges masked.

  2. Prepare

    Filling holes and cracks, sanding, degreasing. New plaster primed first.

  3. Two coats

    Ceiling first, then walls, then the lacquer work. Each coat left to dry properly.

  4. Check

    We check together with a raking light. We only tidy up once you're happy.

Questions about painting

Can I stay at home while you paint?

Yes. Modern water-based wall paint smells very little. We work room by room, so you can use the rest of the house as normal.

How long after plastering can you paint?

Usually after about a week. The plaster must be dry everywhere and light in colour. Then we apply a primer first, otherwise the paint won't bond well.

Do you also paint outside?

We focus on interior work. For exterior painting we can recommend a colleague.
