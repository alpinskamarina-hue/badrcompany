What we do

Smooth, straight and dry on time

You don't notice good plastering. You just see a beautiful wall. That's why preparation and the right base matter as much as the plastering itself.

  • Paint-ready plaster: smooth enough to paint straight away
  • Wallpaper-ready plaster: flat, for wallpaper or glass fibre
  • Textured finishes such as spachtelputz or spray finish on ceilings
  • Repair of cracks, holes and old plaster layers
  • Seamless finishing of new drywall

How we work

Our approach

  1. Cover up

    Floors, frames and sockets are taped off. Furniture is wrapped in plastic.

  2. Prepare

    Loose bits removed, holes filled, and a primer that suits the surface.

  3. Plaster

    Corner beads first, then the whole wall in one go, smoothed to the chosen finish.

  4. Dry and hand over

    We tidy up and tell you how long it needs to dry before you can paint.

Questions about plastering

How long does plaster take to dry?

Allow about a week for a normal coat, with good ventilation and a room that isn't cold. Thick coats or a damp room need longer. Only paint when the wall is evenly light in colour everywhere.

What's the difference between paint-ready and wallpaper-ready?

Paint-ready is smoother, because paint shows every imperfection. Wallpaper-ready is flat but doesn't need to be as smooth, as wallpaper hides small unevenness. Paint-ready therefore takes a little more time.

Can you do just the ceiling?

Yes. We take on small jobs too, with a minimum of 4 hours per job.
