What we do

Joints that line up

With tiling, every mistake stays visible for years. That's why we make a tile plan first: where the first tile starts, where the cuts go and how wall and floor line up.

  • Wall tiles in bathrooms, toilets and kitchens
  • Floor tiles, also over underfloor heating
  • Large-format tiles with a levelling system, no lippage
  • Removing old tiles and levelling the base
  • Grout and silicone in a matching colour

How we work

Our approach

  1. Tile plan

    Together we decide on the pattern and where the cuts go, so it looks right.

  2. Base

    Flat, dry and solid. In wet rooms first a waterproof layer with sealing tape in the corners.

  3. Set

    Full-bed adhesive, levelling system for large tiles. Every row checked with a level.

  4. Grout and seal

    Neat grout lines, corners and joints sealed. Handed over clean.

Questions about tiling

Can you tile over old tiles?

Sometimes, if the old tiles are flat and firmly attached and the extra height is no problem at doors and drains. Removing them is often better though. We'll check during the site visit.

What joint width looks best with large tiles?

With rectified large-format tiles we usually use a 2 to 3 mm joint. That looks calm and clean. Tiles with an uneven edge need a wider joint.

Do you supply the tiles?

You can choose and buy the tiles yourself, or we order them for you. We tell you in advance how many m² you need, including cutting waste.
