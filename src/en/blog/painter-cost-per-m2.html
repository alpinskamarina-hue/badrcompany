Per m² or per hour?

Most painters charge per m² for walls and ceilings and per item for doors and frames. For small or hard-to-measure jobs they often charge by the hour.

A price per m² is usually clearer for you: you know in advance what you'll pay, even if the job takes longer.

What drives the price?

  • Preparation. Filling holes, repairing cracks, sanding and degreasing take time. The worse the surface, the higher the price.
  • Number of coats. A light colour over white often takes two coats, a dark colour or a patchy wall sometimes three.
  • Ceiling or wall. Working overhead is slower, which is why a ceiling costs more per m².
  • Gloss work. Doors, frames and stairs need sanding, masking and fine brushwork. That's why they are priced per item.
  • Size of the job. On a small job, travel and masking weigh more heavily. That's why painters have a minimum charge; Homedeal gives € 300 as the minimum cost.

9% or 21% VAT

For painting a home that is more than 2 years old you pay 9% VAT on labour. Materials and new builds are 21%. Read more in VAT on renovation: 9% or 21%?

How to keep costs down

  • Have several rooms done at once. The minimum charge then weighs less and everything is masked only once.
  • Empty the room yourself. The less there is to move and cover, the sooner the painter can start.
  • Combine with plastering. If you also have walls plastered, the painter can continue right after drying and priming. Read Priming before plastering.
  • Ask for a price per m². Then you know where you stand in advance.

Frequently asked questions

What does a painter cost per hour?

Price guides give about € 30 to € 60 per hour for an interior painter, depending on the source and whether VAT is included. For walls and ceilings, a price per m² is usually clearer.

What does painting cost per m² including materials?

Homedeal gives € 10 to € 16 per m² for wall painting, including two coats of paint, labour and VAT. At Badr Company labour costs € 9 per m² excl. VAT; paint is charged separately.

What does it cost to have a room painted?

For a 4.5 by 3.8 metre room with walls, ceiling, one door and one frame, labour with us comes to about € 697 excl. VAT, or € 759 with 9% VAT. Use the calculator above for your own room.

Why is a ceiling more expensive than a wall?

Working overhead is slower and needs a scaffold or ladder. That's why painters charge a higher price per m² for ceilings.

Do I pay 9% or 21% VAT on painting?

If your home is older than 2 years, you pay 9% VAT on labour. Materials and new builds are 21%.
