"What does plastering cost per square metre?" It's the question we get most often. Here we show exactly how we calculate, so you can check for yourself whether a price makes sense.

Step 1: the hourly rate

We charge € 50 per hour, excluding VAT. Including 9% VAT that is € 54.50 per hour. Travel costs are included, anywhere in the Netherlands. We charge a minimum of 4 hours per job.

Step 2: hours per m²

How much time plastering takes depends on the finish and the surface. Our averages:

ItemTime per m²Labour excl. VAT
Wall, paint-ready0.5 hours€ 25.00
Wall, wallpaper-readyapprox. 0.43 hours€ 21.25
Ceiling, paint-ready0.6 hours€ 30.00

A ceiling takes more time, because you're working overhead. Wallpaper-ready is a little quicker, because it doesn't need to be as smooth.

Step 3: VAT

Plastering a home older than 2 years is 9% VAT. A paint-ready wall then comes to about € 27 per m² including VAT. For new builds or commercial property it's 21%. Read how that works.

Step 4: materials

Plaster, primer and corner beads are charged separately, at cost price. For a normal room that's usually a small part of the total.

Example: a 4.2 × 3.6 metre living room

A room with a 2.6 metre ceiling height, one door and one window:

  • Walls: (4.2 + 3.6) × 2 × 2.6 = 40.6 m², minus door and window ≈ 37.5 m²
  • Ceiling: 4.2 × 3.6 ≈ 15.1 m²
  • Time: 37.5 × 0.5 + 15.1 × 0.6 ≈ 27.8 hours
  • Labour: 27.8 × € 50 = € 1,390 excl. VAT
  • With 9% VAT: about € 1,515

What makes plastering more expensive?

  • A poor surface: loose old plaster, many cracks or crooked walls.
  • Lots of corners, niches or a stairwell.
  • High ceilings that need a mobile scaffold.
  • A room that isn't empty.

Why not a fixed price per m²?

Because every wall is different. A price per m² is a good guide, but the real price depends on the surface. That's why we always come and look for free and then give a fixed price. That price won't change, unless you want to add something yourself.
