What does it cost to plaster walls?

The cost per m² mainly depends on the finish. If you want to paint the wall afterwards, you choose paint-ready plastering. It has to be the smoothest, so it takes the most time. If you are going to hang wallpaper, wallpaper-ready is enough and you pay less.

Torn between plastering and fibreglass wallpaper? Plastering costs more, but lasts for years and gives a cleaner result. We work to a flatness of at most 2 mm per 2 metres and finish corners with corner beads.

Plastering a ceiling: what does it cost?

A ceiling costs € 18 per m² excl. VAT with us. The plasterer works overhead, on scaffolding, and the room has to be covered well. If you have walls and ceiling plastered at the same time, you save on preparation and covering.

What determines the price of plastering?

  • Area. The more m², the more efficient. Small jobs fall under the € 250 minimum.
  • Surface. Removing cracks, loose lime or old textured plaster first takes extra time.
  • Finish. Wallpaper-ready, textured or paint-ready.
  • Access. Stairwells, high ceilings and many corners or window frames.
  • Empty or lived-in. In an empty room we work faster and less needs covering.

How long does plaster need to dry?

That depends on the thickness, temperature and ventilation. As a rule of thumb, allow about a week per centimetre of thickness before you paint.
