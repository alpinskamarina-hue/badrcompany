What does a bathroom cost per m²?

A price per m² for the whole bathroom says little. A toilet, a shower and a vanity cost the same in a 4 m² bathroom as in an 8 m² one. Only the tiling grows with the area. That is why a small bathroom is always more expensive per m² than a large one.

What does go per m² is the tiling: € 35 per m² of wall and € 38 per m² of floor at Badr Company, excl. VAT and tiles. A 2 × 3 metre bathroom with a 2.6 m ceiling has about 24 m² of wall and 6 m² of floor.

How to keep costs under control

  • Keep the layout as it is. If the toilet and shower stay in the same place, less plumbing is needed.
  • Choose a standard tile size. Large format and mosaic take more labour: € 12 or € 20 extra per m².
  • Buy the sanitary ware yourself, but in time. Order everything before the work starts, so the bathroom isn't left half-finished for weeks.
  • A shower instead of a bath. A walk-in shower fits a smaller room and needs no bath panel.
  • One tradesman. No plumber, tiler and plasterer waiting for each other and each sending their own bill.

The order of the work and how long it takes are explained in Bathroom renovation: the right order in 6 steps. For a bathroom of around 5 m², expect two to three weeks.

What VAT do you pay on a bathroom?

On tiling, plumbing and fitting sanitary ware you pay 21% VAT, also in an older home. Only plastering or painting the ceiling falls under 9% in a home older than 2 years. How that works exactly is explained in 9% or 21% VAT on a renovation.

Frequently asked questions

What does a new bathroom cost including installation?

A complete bathroom including materials, installation and VAT usually costs € 8,000–25,000. A basic bathroom starts around € 12,000, a luxury bathroom goes above € 18,000 (Refresh Bathrooms, September 2026).

What does a new 2 by 3 metre bathroom cost?

At Badr Company the labour for a 2 × 3 metre bathroom with demolition, plumbing, tiling and four sanitary items costs about € 6,694 including 21% VAT. Sanitary ware and tiles come on top.

What is a reasonable price for a bathroom?

For an average bathroom of 5 to 8.5 m² with a decent finish, € 12,000–18,000 with everything included is a common amount (Refresh Bathrooms). Always ask for a quote that lists labour and materials per item.

How long does a bathroom renovation take?

For a bathroom of around 5 m², expect two to three weeks. Drying times of the waterproofing layer and the tile adhesive set much of the pace.

What VAT do I pay on a new bathroom?

21% on tiling, plumbing and fitting sanitary ware. Only plastering or painting the ceiling falls under 9% in a home older than 2 years.
