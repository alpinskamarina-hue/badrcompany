What does the material of a PVC floor cost?

The material is the largest part of the bill. The price mainly depends on three things: the thickness of the wear layer, the fitting method and the brand or design.

Source: Vloerenland, material prices excl. fitting (accessed November 2026); ABC Floors (May 2026).

Wear layer and use class. The wear layer is the transparent top layer that protects the design. The packaging also shows a use class. Class 22 is for normal use in a living room, class 23 for heavy use such as a hallway, kitchen or children's room. Classes 31 to 34 are for commercial spaces, with a wear layer of 0.3 to 0.7 mm. With children or pets a wear layer of at least 0.55 mm is the safe choice.

Click or glue-down PVC: which is better?

Click PVC floats on an underlay. Glue-down PVC is glued over the whole floor onto a levelled subfloor. Which one is better depends on your room:

Extra costs to keep in mind

  • Removing the old floor. Taking out laminate or carpet and sanding off glue residue takes extra time. We put it in the quote beforehand.
  • Underlay. With click PVC without a built-in underlay, it comes on top. In an apartment the owners' association (VvE) often requires a sound-dampening underlay.
  • Skirting boards and thresholds. Skirting is charged per metre, thresholds per piece.
  • Cutting waste. Always order a little more than the number of m², especially for herringbone and rooms with many corners.
  • Small area. Floor work has a minimum charge of € 300 excl. VAT per job.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, click or glue-down PVC?

Click PVC is cheaper and faster, because levelling is often not needed. Glue-down PVC is quieter, passes the heat of underfloor heating through better and looks sleeker. With underfloor heating or in an apartment with strict noise rules you usually choose glue-down PVC.

What does a 50 m² PVC floor cost?

For 50 m² of click PVC with mid-range to higher-end material, expect about € 2,800–3,800 including material, fitting, skirting boards and VAT. With glue-down PVC and levelling, add about € 1,300.

What does a PVC floor cost per m² including fitting?

On average € 55–105 per m², including material, levelling and fitting. Click PVC without levelling is at the lower end, glue-down PVC or herringbone at the upper end.

How long does it take to fit a PVC floor?

A click floor in a living room is usually done in one day. With glue-down PVC the levelling compound has to dry first: about 24 hours for a thin layer, 48–72 hours for a thicker layer. Then expect two to three days.

What VAT do I pay on a PVC floor?

On floor work and floor material you pay 21% VAT. The low 9% rate does not apply to floors.
