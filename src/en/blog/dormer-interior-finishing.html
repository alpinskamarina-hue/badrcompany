What does the interior finishing of a dormer include?

A new dormer is usually handed over wind- and watertight. What is and isn't included on the inside differs per builder. Some dormers come with the side walls already lined with plasterboard, others still show the timber studs and insulation. So ask before you sign. Interior finishing covers these parts:

  • The side walls: the two triangular cheeks of the dormer.
  • The ceiling of the dormer.
  • The upstand below the window frame and the window sill.
  • The connection to the existing sloped roof and the knee walls.

The right order in 7 steps

  1. Wind- and watertight. Only start inside once the dormer is fully closed from the outside and no moisture gets in after a heavy shower.
  2. Wiring. Run the cables for sockets, a switch or recessed spotlights before the boards go on. Doing it later costs much more.
  3. Insulation. Fill the space between the studs tightly with insulation, without gaps. Then fix a vapour control foil on the inside, with taped seams.
  4. Plasterboard. Screw plasterboard onto the side walls and the ceiling. Finish the connection to the sloped roof neatly.
  5. Window sill. Fit the window sill under the frame and finish the upstand below it.
  6. Joints and plastering. Fill the joints and skim the walls and ceiling ready for paint. Let the plaster dry well, roughly 1 mm per day.
  7. Priming and painting. Prime the dry plaster, then paint it. How and when is explained in Priming plaster walls.

Insulation: which rules apply?

In the Netherlands, a new dormer on a home has to meet the insulation requirements for new buildings (Besluit bouwwerken leefomgeving, article 5.20(3)). According to the government information point IPLO, that means:

PartRequirement
Roof of the dormerRc at least 6.3 m²·K/W
Each separate closed partRc at least 2.6 m²·K/W
Frame, window or door, eachU at most 2.2 W/m²·K
Windows and frames together, on averageU at most 1.65 W/m²·K

Source: IPLO, Energiezuinigheid: regels bij verbouw and regels bij nieuwbouw (articles 4.152, 4.153 and 5.20 Bbl, accessed 13 November 2026). According to IPLO, dormer side walls may count as an equivalent part under the U-value requirement.

The dormer builder usually takes care of the roof insulation. Ask which Rc value they deliver. If the side walls are not insulated yet, that becomes part of the interior finishing. At the rear of a house you often don't need a permit, but the building rules always apply. More in Dormer without a permit.

Common mistakes

  • Starting too early. If the dormer still leaks, the insulation gets wet and the plasterboard warps.
  • Gaps in the insulation. Every gap is a cold bridge, where condensation and mould can form.
  • Forgetting the foil or not taping it. Moist indoor air then gets into the insulation.
  • Wiring afterwards. Cutting channels in new plasterboard and plaster costs more than running cables first.
  • Painting too soon. Paint on wet plaster peels later. Wait until the plaster is light and even everywhere.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to line the inside of a dormer?

Dutch price comparison sites mention € 450 to € 800 on average for lining the inside, without materials. At Badr Company the interior finishing costs € 900 excl. VAT if we also build the dormer. Otherwise we charge per m².

How do I insulate a dormer from the inside?

Fill the space between the timber studs tightly with insulation, without gaps. Then fix a vapour control foil with taped seams on the warm side and screw plasterboard over it.

What Rc value does a new dormer need?

A new dormer has to meet the new-build requirements: Rc at least 6.3 for the roof and at least 2.6 for each separate closed part. Each window frame may have a U-value of at most 2.2.

Can I finish the inside of a dormer myself?

Yes, if you are handy with insulation and plasterboard. Many people have the plastering done, because a smooth result on the side walls and ceiling is tricky. Always make sure there is a proper vapour control layer.

How long does finishing the inside take?

According to Aannemer Gigant, lining the inside takes about 2 days. After that the plaster has to dry, roughly 1 mm per day, before it can be painted.
