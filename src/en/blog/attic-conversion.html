What does an attic conversion cost?

The price depends mostly on what is already there. If you already have fixed stairs and enough headroom, it is mainly insulating and finishing. If you need stairs, a dormer or a bathroom, the cost rises quickly.

If you insulate, the insulation value counts. According to Homedeal, glass wool must be 14 to 16 cm thick for an Rd of 4.5, PIR about 10 cm. On a small attic, PIR therefore leaves you more space.

The steps in the right order

  1. Structure and plan. Can the floor carry the weight and is there enough headroom? According to Slimster, the floor of a loft space (vliering) usually needs strengthening. If you want to change beams or a load-bearing wall, have a structural engineer check it.
  2. Stairs. A loft ladder is not enough for a bedroom. Fixed stairs cost € 1,500 to € 3,500, depending on the shape.
  3. Daylight. A roof window or a dormer. A dormer also gives more headroom and floor space.
  4. Insulation and vapour barrier. Insulation between or on top of the rafters, with vapour barrier foil on the warm side.
  5. Cables and pipes. Electrics, heating and ventilation if needed, before the walls are closed.
  6. Knee walls, walls and ceiling. Plasterboard on metal studs or timber battens. How that works is explained in Metal stud wall.
  7. Joints, plastering or spray finish. See Finishing plasterboard.
  8. Floor, door and smoke alarm. The floor goes in last, so it doesn't get damaged.

Permits and rules

  • Interior work. If you only refit the attic, you do not need to apply for a permit, according to Slimster. The municipality of Utrecht points out that the building rules of the Bbl (Besluit bouwwerken leefomgeving) still apply.
  • Load-bearing structure. If you remove a load-bearing wall or beams, it is no longer a permit-free job. Run the permit check on the Omgevingsloket and contact a building expert.
  • Roof window. At the back, according to IPLO, a roof window needs no permit if it sticks out no more than 0.6 m from the roof surface and stays more than 0.5 m from the roof edges. At the front it may not stick out of the roof surface. For a listed building or in a protected townscape a permit is often needed.
  • Dormer. Often permit-free at the back, not at the front. Read Dormer without a permit.
  • Smoke alarm. Since 1 July 2022, a smoke alarm is mandatory on every floor with a bedroom, living room or kitchen. An attic with a bedroom counts. The owner is responsible.

Sources: Slimster, zolder verbouwen tot slaapkamer (20 January 2025); municipality of Utrecht, woning aan de binnenkant verbouwen (updated 3 November 2026); IPLO, vergunningvrije dakramen (updated 20 November 2025); Dutch government, hoeveel rookmelders moet ik in mijn huis hebben (updated 15 October 2026). How to keep costs down

  • One company for everything. Homedeal says a complete package from one contractor is cheaper than separate tradespeople.
  • Plan insulation, electrics and plasterboard walls in one phase. Then nothing needs to be opened twice.
  • Spray finish instead of plaster. € 5 – € 9 per m² versus € 15 – € 35 per m² (Homedeal). Smooth plaster looks better in raking light under a roof window.
  • A roof window instead of a dormer. A roof window costs € 700 – € 900, a dormer € 7,000 – € 9,800 (Homedeal). But you get less headroom.
  • Subsidy. Roof insulation with Rd 3.5 or higher may qualify for the ISDE subsidy, € 4 to € 8 per m² according to Homedeal, with at least two insulation measures.
  • Ask for three quotes and have them broken down per item.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to make a bedroom in the attic?

According to Homedeal usually € 12,000 to € 20,000 for an extra bedroom, with a dormer, a plasterboard wall and stairs with a quarter turn. If there are already stairs and you choose a roof window, € 8,000 to € 12,000 can be enough.

Do I need a permit to convert my attic?

Usually not for refitting the attic. You do if you change the load-bearing structure, for a dormer at the front, or for a roof window that doesn't meet the rules or is in a listed building. If in doubt, run the permit check on the Omgevingsloket.

How long does an attic conversion take?

Homedeal gives 1 to 2 weeks for a basic conversion, 3 to 4 weeks for a bedroom with a dormer and 4 to 8 weeks for a luxury attic.

Is a smoke alarm mandatory in the attic?

Yes, if there is a bedroom in the attic. Since 1 July 2022 every floor with a bedroom, living room or kitchen must have a smoke alarm. An attic used only for storage does not need one, according to the Dutch government.

What do you use to clad an attic?

Usually plasterboard on metal studs or timber battens. Then the joints are finished and everything is plastered or spray finished. Plasterboard is cheap, straight and easy to insulate.
