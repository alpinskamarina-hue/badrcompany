The VAT rate can make a real difference on a renovation. For part of the work you pay 9% VAT instead of 21%. But not for everything, and only under certain conditions. Here's an overview.

Which jobs are 9%?

According to the Dutch Tax Administration (Belastingdienst), the reduced 9% rate applies to these jobs on a home older than 2 years:

  • Painting walls, ceilings, frames and doors
  • Plastering walls and ceilings
  • Wallpapering
  • Insulating the home

For plastering, the Belastingdienst says the preparation, pre-treatment and materials used also fall under 9%.

Which jobs are 21%?

Work that is too far removed from these four jobs falls under the standard 21% rate. For plastering, the Belastingdienst itself gives these examples:

  • installing drywall partitions
  • tiling walls
  • laying floors

Fitting bathroom fixtures, plumbing and building a dormer are also 21%.

ExampleA new bathroom consists of several parts. Plastering the ceiling is 9%, tiling and fixtures are 21%. A good quote shows the rate for each item.

When is a home older than 2 years?

The 2-year period starts on the date the property is first used as a home. What counts is its age at the time the work is done. A new-build home that's eighteen months old is therefore still 21%.

If the home was built in stages, for example with a new extension, at least 50% of it must consist of parts older than 2 years. You can check the age in the Dutch Basic Registration of Addresses and Buildings (BAG) at the Kadaster.

What does this mean for your wallet?

A worked example using our hourly rate of € 50 excluding VAT. Plastering a living room takes about 20 hours of labour:

9% VAT21% VAT
Labour excl. VAT€ 1,000€ 1,000
VAT€ 90€ 210
Total€ 1,090€ 1,210

That's € 120 difference on this job. For plastering and painting a whole house, it quickly adds up.

What to watch out for

  • Ask for a quote that shows the VAT rate for each item.
  • Be careful with "cash, no receipt" offers. You'll have no warranty and no invoice if something goes wrong.
  • The reduced rate only applies to homes. For commercial property you pay 21%.
