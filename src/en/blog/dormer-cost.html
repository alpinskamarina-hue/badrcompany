Price by width

Suppliers do not charge per m², but by the width of the dormer. The wider, the more expensive, but per metre it gets cheaper: the fixed costs of installation, crane and finishing are spread over more metres.

Prefab or custom-built?

Prefab is usually cheaper and faster. Custom-built is useful with an unusual roof pitch, a special size, or if you want the dormer to match an old house exactly. All three suppliers we looked at install a prefab dormer in one day.

What comes on top?

  • Permit. Many dormers at the back of the house need no permit. Read A dormer without a permit: when is it allowed? first. If you do need a permit, Gelders Dakkapel charges € 575 to € 650 for the application, excluding the municipal fees. According to Wiko, those fees are often € 200 to € 500.
  • Building drawing. Dakkapellenprof charges € 279 excluding VAT.
  • Scaffolding and traffic plan. Gelders Dakkapel names these as costs that vary per address.
  • Interior finishing. After installation, the cheeks and ceiling are still bare inside. How that works and what it costs is in Finishing a dormer from the inside.

Sources: Gelders Dakkapel (updated 28 September 2026); Wiko Dakkapellen; Dakkapellenprof (both accessed 25 November 2026).

Frequently asked questions

What does a 4-metre dormer cost?

€ 7,490 at Dakkapellenprof for a prefab plastic dormer, from € 8,700 at Gelders Dakkapel and € 9,840 at Wiko. Dakkapellenprof and Gelders state their prices include installation and VAT.

What does a dormer cost per metre?

Suppliers charge by width, not per metre. On average, a 3 to 5 metre prefab dormer at Dakkapellenprof works out at about € 1,700 to € 2,100 per metre of width. The wider, the lower the price per metre.

How long does it take to install a dormer?

All three suppliers we looked at install a prefab dormer in one day. A traditional dormer takes three days on average, according to Dakkapellenprof. The interior finishing comes after that.

Do I need a permit for a dormer?

Often not at the back, usually yes at the front or side. Read the rules in our article on a dormer without a permit. Municipal fees are often € 200 to € 500, according to Wiko.

Is a custom-built dormer more expensive than prefab?

Yes, by € 500 to € 2,000 according to Dakkapellenprof. Custom-built is useful for a special size, an unusual roof pitch or an old house.
