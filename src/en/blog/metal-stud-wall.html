What is a metal stud wall?

A metal stud wall is a frame of galvanised steel profiles, clad on one or both sides with plasterboard. You use it for two kinds of wall:

  • A lining wall: an extra wall just in front of an existing one. Useful against a crooked, cold or damaged wall, or to hide pipes.
  • A partition wall: a free-standing wall that divides a room, for example for an extra bedroom or a workspace. It is clad on both sides.

No wet trades are involved, apart from finishing the joints. A wall is often up within a day.

Structure and wall thickness

Profiles come in 50, 75 and 100 mm widths. The wider the profile, the taller and sturdier the wall and the more insulation fits in. Two layers of plasterboard per side make the wall quieter and more fire resistant.

For comparison, Metalstudwand.com lists 30 minutes of fire resistance and 43 dB of sound insulation for a 100 mm wall with one board per side and insulation. With two boards per side it is 60 minutes and 50 dB.

We almost always work with metal stud. The wall stays straight, the joints crack less and it suits smooth plastering better. If something heavy goes on the wall, such as a TV or kitchen cabinets, we fit a timber batten inside the wall beforehand.

How to build a metal stud wall

  1. Setting out. Mark the position of the wall on the floor and ceiling, preferably with a laser, so the wall is square and plumb.
  2. U-tracks. Screw or plug the U-tracks to the floor and ceiling. Gyproc recommends acoustic tape under all profiles that touch the existing structure.
  3. C-studs. Place the upright C-studs into the U-tracks every 60 cm, all with the open side facing the same way. Cut them about 1 cm shorter than the height.
  4. Clad the first side. Screw the plasterboards to the studs, at most 25 cm between screws and at least 1 cm from the edge. Cut the boards about 1 cm shorter than the floor-to-ceiling height.
  5. Wiring and insulation. Run the cables through the pre-punched holes in the studs and fill the wall with stone wool or glass wool.
  6. Second side. Clad the other side, with the joints staggered relative to the first side.
  7. Finish the joints. Tape the joints with mesh tape and fill them and the screw holes in two thin coats. Then plaster, wallpaper or paint.

Sources: Gamma, Gyproc wand plaatsen met metal stud; Metalstudwand.com, Handleiding metalstud wanden plaatsen (accessed 14 November 2026). According to Gamma, a free-standing Gyproc lining wall may be at most 2.4 m high; taller walls must be braced against the wall behind.

Downsides and points to watch

  • Not load-bearing. A metal stud wall doesn't carry a floor or roof. If you want to replace a load-bearing wall, you need a structural engineer.
  • Sound. A single wall without insulation stops little sound. Insulation and a second layer of board make a big difference.
  • Heavy items. A TV, kitchen cabinets or a washbasin need reinforcement in the wall in advance.
  • Damp rooms. In a bathroom you use moisture-resistant plasterboard.

Frequently asked questions

What are the downsides of a metal stud wall?

The wall is not load-bearing, stops little sound without insulation and needs reinforcement for heavy items. Insulation, double boarding and timber battens in the right places largely solve that.

Which is cheaper, timber or metal stud?

Timber battens are often slightly cheaper per metre. Metal stud is straighter, lighter and doesn't move, so the finish cracks less easily.

How far apart are the studs?

The upright C-studs are placed every 60 cm, centre to centre. That way every joint of a 1200 mm wide plasterboard falls on a stud.

Which insulation goes between metal studs?

Usually stone wool or glass wool. It fills the space between the studs and dampens sound. Put the insulation in together with the wiring, before the second side is closed.

What does a metal stud wall cost per m²?

According to Tussenwandplaatsen.nl, € 45 to € 120 per m² on average, depending on insulation and boarding. At Badr Company labour costs € 39 per m² for a lining wall and € 54 per m² for a partition wall, including 21% VAT.
