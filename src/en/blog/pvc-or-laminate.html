How are they built?

Laminate has a wood-fibre core. That makes it look real and feel solid, but it swells if water stands on it for long. PVC is entirely synthetic: thinner, softer and water-resistant. PVC does need a stable, smooth subfloor; it can't simply go on a wooden plank floor, and glue-down PVC always goes on a levelled floor.

Sources: Parketloods (3 April 2026); Voordeelvloeren, PVC of laminaat vloer; Floorlife (5 May 2026).

Water and moisture

PVC is very resistant to moisture and water and suits kitchens, bathrooms, hallways and utility rooms. Laminate is sensitive to prolonged moisture and belongs in dry rooms, unless you choose water-resistant laminate. Even that usually offers less protection than PVC, according to the Dutch retailer Kwantum.

Noise

PVC is naturally quiet. Laminate makes more footstep noise, especially without a good underlay. If you live in an apartment, check what your owners' association (VvE) requires; Kwantum gives a minimum of 10 dB sound reduction as an example. A PVC floor or underlay with a 10 dB certificate meets that, according to Voordeelvloeren.

Underfloor heating

Both floors can go on underfloor heating, but PVC conducts heat better. Watch the thermal resistance of floor and underlay together: it must stay below 0.14 m²K/W. Voordeelvloeren gives two examples: 8 mm laminate with underlay comes to 0.065 m²K/W, 5 mm PVC with underlay to 0.039 m²K/W. Parketloods mentions a maximum floor temperature of usually 27 – 28 °C.

Which floor for which room?

  • Kitchen, bathroom, hallway, utility room: PVC. Water and wet shoes do it no harm.
  • Living room with underfloor heating: PVC, because of its low thermal resistance.
  • Apartment: PVC or laminate with an underlay that meets the VvE requirement.
  • Bedroom, study: laminate is fine here and usually cheaper.

Frequently asked questions

Which is better, PVC or laminate?

It depends on the room. PVC is better in wet rooms, with underfloor heating and if you want a quiet floor. Laminate is usually cheaper and fine in dry rooms.

Which floor is better with underfloor heating?

PVC, because it lets heat through better. Both work as long as floor and underlay together stay below 0.14 m²K/W thermal resistance.

How long do PVC and laminate last?

Parketloods gives 15 to 25 years for PVC and 10 to 20 years for laminate. Trustoo gives an average of 10 to 15 years for laminate.

How much more expensive is PVC than laminate?

According to Voordeelvloeren, PVC is on average 50% more expensive than comparable laminate. At Badr Company, fitting click PVC costs € 2 per m² more; glue-down PVC also needs levelling.

Can laminate go in the bathroom?

Ordinary laminate can't: it is sensitive to prolonged moisture. Water-resistant laminate exists, but PVC usually offers more protection.
