What does renovating a toilet cost?

The price depends mainly on how much changes. If the toilet stays in the same place and you tile halfway up the wall, the cost stays low. Tiles up to the ceiling, moving pipes or a more expensive toilet quickly push it up.

Toilet Direct quotes € 3,250 for a basic package with floor, walls up to 1.20 m and a standard wall-hung toilet, and € 3,600 with a hand basin and accessories. Tiles up to the ceiling cost € 450 to € 600 extra there. A cheaper route is wall panels over the old tiles: Bewust Sanitair charges from € 95 per m² including fitting and VAT.

The order in 9 steps

  1. Turn off the water and demolish. The old toilet, basin and tiles come out. If the old tiles are still firmly attached, Praxis says you can also tile over them.
  2. Level the floor if it is not flat. Read more in Floor levelling.
  3. Plaster the walls and ceiling, especially above the tiles.
  4. Mount the concealed cistern and connect it to water and drainage.
  5. Build the boxing around the cistern, usually plasterboard on a frame.
  6. Tile the walls and floor.
  7. Hang the toilet.
  8. Hang the hand basin. Check beforehand which side the tap hole is on.
  9. Seal along the floor, toilet and basin.

Source: Praxis, Hulp per klus: toilet. For the order of a whole bathroom, read Renovating a bathroom: the right order.

How long does it take?

According to Sanidirect, a professional can renovate a toilet in one day. Toilet Direct works with two days: demolition on day 1, fitting and finishing on day 2. If you hire separate tradespeople, Toilet Direct counts on three to five working days, because plaster, adhesive and grout need to dry in between. Make sure you can use another toilet during that time.

Sources: Sanidirect, Toilet renovatie: kosten en stappenplan; Toilet Direct, Wat kost een toilet renovatie? (both consulted November 2026).

Wall-hung or floor-standing toilet?

A wall-hung toilet saves space and is easy to clean, Praxis writes: the floor underneath stays free. It does need a concealed cistern inside a boxing, and that cistern is sold separately. A floor-standing toilet (close-coupled) is easier to install but harder to keep clean. Only want a new toilet while the floor and walls are still fine? Toilet Direct charges € 600 to € 1,400 for the toilet, concealed cistern and fitting together.

The boxing around the cistern is a small lining wall. Read how such a wall is built in Building a metal stud wall.

How to keep costs down

  • Keep the toilet in the same place. Then little pipework is usually needed, according to Praxis.
  • Tile up to 1.20 m and plaster the wall above. Tiles up to the ceiling cost € 450 to € 600 extra at Toilet Direct. In our calculator, for a WC of 1.40 × 0.90 m, it saves about € 235 in labour.
  • Tile over the old tiles if they are still firmly attached, or choose wall panels.
  • Floor and walls still fine? Then replace only the toilet and cistern.

Frequently asked questions

What does renovating a toilet cost on average?

Praxis gives € 1,200 to € 3,500 on average, with about € 2,500 for an average renovation. Complete packages cost € 3,250 to € 5,000 including materials, labour and VAT, according to Toilet Direct.

How long does a toilet renovation take?

A professional can do it in one day, according to Sanidirect; Toilet Direct counts two days. With separate tradespeople and drying time it takes three to five working days.

In what order do you renovate a toilet?

Turn off the water and demolish, level the floor, plaster the walls and ceiling, mount the concealed cistern, build the boxing, tile, hang the toilet and basin, and seal last.

Can I tile over the old tiles?

Yes, if the old tiles are still firmly attached. That saves demolition work. If they are loose or sound hollow, they have to come off.

Is a wall-hung toilet better than a floor-standing one?

A wall-hung toilet saves space and is easier to clean, but needs a concealed cistern in a boxing. A floor-standing toilet is easier to install.
