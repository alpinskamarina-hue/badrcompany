Short answer: a dropped ceiling (in Dutch: verlaagd plafond) is a new ceiling below the old one, usually plasterboard on a metal stud frame. The frame is fixed to the walls, so the new ceiling hangs free of the old one. A professional installs it in 1 to 3 days. According to the Dutch builder Veldhuisbouw (2026), a basic plasterboard ceiling costs € 55 to € 75 per m² including installation, and € 85 to € 130 per m² with recessed spotlights.

Why a dropped ceiling?

A dropped ceiling solves several things at once:

  • an uneven, cracked or messy old ceiling disappears without demolition;
  • pipes, cables and ventilation ducts are hidden behind it;
  • you can fit recessed spotlights;
  • there is room for insulation, against noise from upstairs neighbours or cold from an attic;
  • a tall room feels smaller and cosier.

The ceiling does come down, though. Measure in advance how much headroom you keep, especially under a beam or near a door that opens inwards.

How is the frame built?

With a self-supporting ceiling, the frame does not hang from the old ceiling; it is fixed only to the walls. A U-shaped edge profile goes along the two long walls, a C-profile along the short walls. Between the long walls you clamp C-profiles at a fixed spacing. The closer together the profiles are, the longer the distance they can span freely.

If the room is wider than the profiles can span, you need wider profiles or an extra suspension in the middle, for example a strip of plywood with a profile on each side. According to Hubo, wider profiles bridge up to 480 cm. Always follow the table from the manufacturer of your profiles.

How to build a dropped ceiling

  1. Set the height. Mark a line all round on every wall with a laser or spirit level.
  2. Fix the edge profiles along the two long walls, every 60 cm. Screw the profiles along the short walls every 125 cm.
  3. Fit the intermediate profiles. Cut them about 1 cm shorter than the distance between the walls, clamp them into the edge profiles and fix them with self-drilling screws. Every 30 cm if the boards run lengthwise, every 50 cm if they run across.
  4. Lay cables and insulation. Wiring for spotlights and any insulation go on or in the frame before the boards.
  5. Screw on the plasterboard, at least every 25 cm with plasterboard screws. A board lift makes this much easier.
  6. Finish. Joints with mesh tape and filler, then sand, prime and paint or plaster.

Sources: Gamma Belgium, Gyproc Metal Stud plafond plaatsen; Hubo, Hoe een verlaagd plafond plaatsen? (both accessed November 2026). More on finishing: Finishing plasterboard. You use the same profiles for a metal stud wall.

Spotlights, insulation and the bathroom

  • Spotlights. Decide where the spots go before the boards are fitted, so the cables are ready and no spot lands on a profile. Veldhuisbouw charges € 35 to € 75 per spot, including fitting, cabling and switching.
  • Insulation. Lay glass wool or rock wool on the frame before the boards go underneath. According to Veldhuisbouw, a dropped ceiling with insulation costs € 90 to € 140 per m².
  • Bathroom. Choose moisture-resistant plasterboard or plastic ceiling panels, and make sure there is good ventilation. Bewust Sanitair usually fits a bathroom ceiling in one day.

Sources: Veldhuisbouw, Verlaagd plafond kosten 2026 (updated May 2026); Bewust Sanitair, Verlaagd plafond badkamer (updated 21 October 2026).

What does a dropped ceiling cost?

Veldhuisbouw works through an example for a 5 × 6 metre living room (30 m²): basic about € 1,950, paint-ready about € 2,550, and with spotlights and wiring about € 3,300. Installation takes 1 to 3 days, depending on the area. Removing an old dropped ceiling costs € 20 to € 50 per m² excluding VAT, according to OfferteAdviseur.

Sources: Veldhuisbouw, Verlaagd plafond kosten 2026 (updated May 2026); OfferteAdviseur, Kosten plafond verhogen (8 October 2025).

Frequently asked questions

What does a dropped ceiling cost per m²?

According to Veldhuisbouw, a basic plasterboard ceiling costs € 55 to € 75 per m² including installation, paint-ready € 70 to € 95 and with recessed spotlights € 85 to € 130 per m². At Badr Company it is € 38 per m² plus € 6 for the joints, excluding VAT.

How long does it take to install a dropped ceiling?

1 to 3 days according to Veldhuisbouw, depending on the area. Plastering and drying come on top of that. A bathroom ceiling is often done in one day.

Does a dropped ceiling have to hang from the old ceiling?

Not always. A self-supporting frame is fixed only to the walls. According to Hubo, profiles every 30 cm span up to 255 cm. A wider room needs wider profiles or an extra suspension.

Is it worth insulating above a dropped ceiling?

Yes, if you are bothered by noise from above or cold from an unheated attic. Insulation goes on the frame before the boards; adding it later is difficult.

Which material suits a dropped ceiling in the bathroom?

Moisture-resistant plasterboard, or plastic ceiling panels that need no painting. In both cases, make sure there is good ventilation.
