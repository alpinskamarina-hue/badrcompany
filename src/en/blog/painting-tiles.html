Where can you paint tiles?

Tile paint sticks to glazed tiles if you prepare the surface properly. But paint is never as hard as fired ceramic, writes the Dutch tile shop De Budget Tegels. It is more sensitive to scratches and knocks. That is why the location matters so much.

The Dutch DIY chains GAMMA and Colora describe tile paint for bathrooms and kitchens as easy to clean and suitable for damp rooms. De Budget Tegels is stricter: wall tiles in the kitchen and in the dry parts of the bathroom are fine to paint, but they strongly advise against painting floor tiles or tiles in the shower. There the paint wears and peels quickly.

Painting tiles in 5 steps

  1. Degrease. Scrub tiles and grout clean with degreaser and rinse. Let everything dry well.
  2. Remove silicone, replace broken tiles. Cut out old sealant, because paint does not stick to it. Replace cracked tiles first.
  3. Sand lightly. Dull the glaze with fine sandpaper and wipe off the dust with a damp cloth.
  4. Bonding primer. Apply a primer for smooth surfaces. Paint the edges and grout first with a brush, then the tiles with a roller. Colora lets the primer dry for 6 hours.
  5. Two coats of tile paint. Let it dry between coats as the tin says; GAMMA mentions at least 24 hours. Pull off the tape while the paint is wet. Let the paint cure for a few days before it gets wet, and only then apply new sealant.

Sources: GAMMA, Hoe tegels verven; Colora, Tegels schilderen in 5 stappen; De Budget Tegels (20 April 2026).

Clean painted tiles with a soft cloth and mild soap. Scouring sponges and bleach damage the paint layer.

When to choose new tiles

  • In the shower and on the floor. Paint is not a lasting solution there.
  • With loose, hollow or cracked tiles. Paint hides the colour, not the damage.
  • If you are renovating anyway. If pipes, sanitary ware or the layout change, the tiles often have to come off anyway.
  • If it has to last. Ceramic is harder and more wear-resistant than a layer of paint.

Frequently asked questions

Can you paint over tiles?

Yes, on wall tiles in dry spots, such as around the toilet, the washbasin or as a kitchen splashback. Degrease and sand the tiles, apply a bonding primer and then two coats of tile paint. It is not recommended in the shower or on the floor.

What paint sticks to tiles?

A special tile paint or a 2-component lacquer, on a bonding primer for smooth surfaces. Don't use an ordinary wall primer, but a special tile primer.

How well does tile paint work in the shower?

Not well. De Budget Tegels strongly advises against it: in the shower the paint peels quickly. New tiles are the better choice there.

What does it cost to have tiles painted?

Slimster gives € 10 to € 50 per m² for primer and tile paint, including materials, labour and VAT. New tiles cost € 50 to € 300 per m² including tiles and labour according to Homedeal.

Can you still see painted grout?

The grout is painted along with the tiles. That makes the wall look flatter, because the difference between tile and joint stands out less.
