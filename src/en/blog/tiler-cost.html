Most tilers charge per m². For small jobs or complex work they charge per hour or per day. These are the amounts several Dutch sources give in 2025 and 2026, for labour only, without tiles:

Why one m² costs more than another

  • Wall or floor. A wall has more edge work: corners, pipes, taps and sockets. That is why Plannr prices a m² of wall higher than a m² of floor.
  • Size. Small tiles and mosaic mean far more joints and cutting. Large tiles are heavy and need a very flat base. Homedeal says tiles from 60 × 60 to 120 × 120 are closer to € 40 than € 30 per hour.
  • Pattern. Herringbone, diagonal or offset laying takes more time and more waste. Plannr adds 10 to 25% for corners and cutting.
  • Base. An uneven floor or wall has to be straightened first. That is work before the tiling starts.
  • Size of the job. A small toilet has many corners on few m². That is why many tilers then charge per day or use a minimum.

How many m² does a tiler lay per day?

This explains the price differences: a tiler earns roughly the same per day, but lays far fewer m² on a wall or with mosaic. So a m² of wall or mosaic costs more.

What comes on top?

  • Tiles. Ceramic tiles cost € 9 to € 82 per m² according to Homedeal, natural stone € 25 to € 160 per m².
  • Cutting waste. Buy extra tiles: Homedeal counts 5% for straight laying, 10% for complex work and 10 to 15% for patterns or large tiles.
  • Adhesive and grout. Roughly € 7 to € 12 per m², according to Plannr.
  • Base. Levelling or preparation costs € 10 to € 20 per m² according to Mondain. More in Floor levelling.
  • Removing the old floor. Demolition and disposal cost € 10 to € 25 per m² according to Mondain.
  • VAT. Tiling is charged at 21% VAT, also in an existing home. Read more in 9% or 21% VAT on a renovation.

Sources: Homedeal (12 November 2025); Plannr (12 May 2026); Mondain (March 2026).

How to keep costs down

  • Choose a standard size and lay it straight. That saves cutting and time.
  • Do the demolition yourself. You can often remove old tiles yourself. Homedeal puts the saving at € 30 to € 40 per hour.
  • Combine jobs. Have walls and floor, or bathroom and toilet, done in one go. Then the minimum counts only once.
  • Ask for a fixed price. With a fixed price per m² you know in advance where you stand. With an hourly or daily rate it depends on the pace.

Frequently asked questions

What does a tiler cost per m²?

According to Homedeal, € 25 to € 40 per m² for labour on average. Plannr gives € 35 to € 55 per m² for a regular floor and € 50 to € 85 for a wall. At Badr Company it is € 38 per m² floor and € 35 per m² wall, excluding VAT, with a surcharge for large or small tiles.

What is a tiler's hourly rate?

Homedeal gives € 30 to € 40 per hour, Mondain € 40 to € 60 for private clients. Per day, a tiler charges € 350 to € 500 according to Plannr.

How many m² does a tiler lay per day?

According to Plannr, 8 to 12 m² for a standard floor, 6 to 9 m² for wall tiles and 3 to 4 m² for small format or mosaic. Homedeal gives 15 to 20 m² for floors in a spacious room.

Are the tiles included in the price per m²?

Usually not. The price per m² is labour. Tiles, adhesive and grout come on top, as does preparing the base.

How much VAT do I pay on tiling?

Tiling is charged at 21% VAT, also in an existing home. The low 9% rate applies to plastering and painting, among other things, in homes older than two years.
